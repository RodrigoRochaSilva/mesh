How to set a password for a Google account

If you originally signed up to AgroXeque with Google (without creating a password), set a password from your AgroXeque account to access mexe.

1

Sign in to AgroXeque

Use your Google account to sign in

2

Open "My Account"

From the AgroXeque platform menu

3

Click "Change my password"

An email will be sent to you

4

Check your email and click the reset link

Check both your inbox and spam folder

5

Set your new password

On the reset page, enter and confirm your new password
